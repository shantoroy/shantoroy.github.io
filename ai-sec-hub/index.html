Frequently asked questions

What is the AI/CyberSec Hub?

A single page that aggregates the latest AI, cybersecurity and AI-security news, blogs, arXiv papers, community discussions and GitHub projects, with a curated resource directory.

What is the difference between the AI Security, AI and Cybersecurity tabs?

AI Security covers securing and attacking AI systems (LLMs, agents, prompt injection, red teaming). AI covers the broader AI landscape. Cybersecurity covers general security news and research. Items can appear in more than one tab.

Which sources are used?

Public RSS feeds from security and AI publications and blogs, the arXiv API, Hacker News, Lobsters and the GitHub search API. Every item links to its original source.

How often is it updated?

The hub refreshes automatically about every 8 hours.

How are the summaries and briefings made?

Short summaries and the daily briefing are written by an AI model from titles and snippets, and may contain mistakes. Always check the linked source.
