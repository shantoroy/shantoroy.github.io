## 42 State AGs Try to Stop Character.AI From Selling Users' Chats
A coalition of 42 state attorneys general is seeking to block Character.AI from selling user data during its Chapter 11 bankruptcy. They say the planned auction would include the private chat histories of more than 21 million U.S. users. The case shows how conversational data can become a liability when a company fails.

Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1042620/character-ai-sale-state-ags-block-selling-users-chats)

## Nvidia Pauses Chip Shipments to Southeast Asia
Nvidia has told manufacturing partners to halt server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia. The move comes as the U.S. tightens efforts to keep AI chips out of China.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/nvidia-hits-pause-on-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia/)

Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-warns-gpt-62-astra-will-hit-usage-limits-40-percent-faster-because-it-fixed-a-bug/)

Simon Willison comments on a report that the New York Times told its photographers not to update Photoshop because of its AI features.
