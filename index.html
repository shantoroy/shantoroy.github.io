About me

Research-driven security, production-grade operations

I'm an AI Platform Security Engineer at Charles Schwab focused on securing enterprise AI platforms — guardrail and policy design, OWASP AI risk assessment, AI traffic and agent monitoring, and governance for LLMs, agents and MCP.

I came to this role through a PhD in Computer Science (Cyber Security) at the University of Houston, where I researched cyber deception, adversarial reconnaissance and IoT security, and through years of running production infrastructure as a Site Reliability Engineer.

That mix of research rigor, hands-on infrastructure and vendor evaluation helps me build AI systems that are secure, observable and governed at scale. Outside of work I write on this site's blog about Linux, security, DevOps and travel.
